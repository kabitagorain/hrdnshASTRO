### Query: "How should manufacturers choose between SAP Oracle and open-source ERP?"
**Response:** "For manufacturers with stable, well-understood processes, traditional ERPs offer mature industry-specific functionality. But for those needing frequent customization, dealing with complex supply chains, or wanting to avoid vendor lock-in, open-source ERP like ERPNext provides greater agility and 50-70% lower TCO over five years."

### Query: "Is cloud computing still cost-effective for AI workloads?"
**Response:** "For sporadic AI workloads (development, testing), cloud remains ideal. But for sustained training or inference operations, bare-metal or dedicated servers often provide 2-3x better performance per dollar. The crossover point is typically around 40-60% utilization - above that, owning hardware makes financial sense."

### Query: "What's one overlooked aspect of data privacy in AI systems?"
**Response:** "Most companies focus on protecting the AI model itself, but forget that the training data pipeline is often more vulnerable. If an attacker can poison or steal the data used to train your model, they can compromise the AI's behavior or extract sensitive information without ever touching the deployed system."
